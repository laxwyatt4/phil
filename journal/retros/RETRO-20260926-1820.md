  "resolver-source/count-lag" (10:15Z, 0.96 vs about 0.985 when I
  re-derive the note's numbers at sd ~0.3M; that is my reconstruction,
  not a recorded read). Each tail cost 0.001-0.002 of Brier against a
  liquid book that was already near-certain. These are unmeasured shades
  under a different name. They were recorded after the DEEP-2026-09-26
  rule existed, so they are **compliance misses**, not a new failure
  mode.

Verdict: estimate wrong early (pace model), then small unmeasured-shade
losses late. Variance was not the driver.
